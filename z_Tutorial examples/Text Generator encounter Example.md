# Encounter Name: Yeti Ambush

The party is traveling through a narrow, snow-covered pass in the [[Icewind Dale]]. The wind howls around them, making it difficult to hear anything beyond a few feet. As they trudge through the knee-deep snow, they suddenly come across a small cave entrance hidden by an overhanging ledge of ice.

Curiosity getting the better of them, the party decides to investigate the cave. Inside, they find a small chamber with a low ceiling and icicles hanging from above. The air is bitterly cold and their breath hangs in front of them.

As they cautiously explore further, they notice movement in the shadows. A pack of Yetis emerges from the darkness, their white fur blending in with the snowy surroundings. The creatures snarl and bare their sharp teeth at the intruders.

The party braces themselves for a fight as the Yetis charge forward, eager to protect their territory.
