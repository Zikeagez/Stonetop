



# Inn Name: The Dragon's Rest

Welcome to The Dragon's Rest, a cozy inn nestled in the heart of a bustling fantasy town. Our inn is known for its warm hospitality and comfortable accommodations, making it the perfect place for weary travelers to rest and rejuvenate.

## Inn Keeper Name: Gideon Ironheart

Gideon Ironheart, the innkeeper of The Dragon's Rest, is a stout and jovial dwarf with a long, silver beard that reaches down to his belt. Known for his friendly nature and love for storytelling, Gideon ensures that every guest feels welcome and at home during their stay. With his vast knowledge of local lore and legends, he often regales patrons with tales of heroic adventures and mythical creatures.
