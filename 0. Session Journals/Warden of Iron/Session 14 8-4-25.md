Professor Vex awakens me before the  sun rises and guides me through the forest a bit. It is misty quiet and dark.

She asks if I have seen dark magics

Being aloof I ask if she means the wine.

But she says no, something deeper. I see it in your eyes. She feels we are kindred in some sense. She pulls a tree branch away and in the clearing we see the results of a profane ritual - a pentagonal array of runes on the ground. Grizzly remains everywhere.  There is an imprint of a body in the center but the body is gone. 

Occult roll: 25 - What I see is very similar to what happened to my pack. 

I ask how long this has been here, and Vex says this "is their latest" a day and a half ago perhaps. 

I ask do you know where they went?
She does not. But this is not the first site I have found. 

They call themselves The Remnants.

She too has personal reasons to pursue them.  They slew her mother and unborn sibling. They came with promises, and in a way those promises were kept. 

They may have reached the peak of their power years ago and have now regained their strength. Whatever is happening to this forest, they see it as a chance to gather more power.
