# Session Overview

%% I like to keep a quick summary of sessions here. %%

[[Daloreth]] is updated that we are being 'arrested' for possessing contraband. But we might be able to come to an agreement.  We are brought to a [[The League of Miracles|League of Miracles]] warehouse. Some [[Wardens of Syngorn]] are present, but they are clearly associated too. 

He asks if we experienced anything weird when interacting with the crystal. [[Luceran]] says that he did which is why it has piqued his interest. 

The Rimelord - Errevon and the Elemental invasion came from Aravon. Luceran thinks the earthen elementals are similar to what happened before the Rimelord arrived. The catalyst could be a similar means to start a similar coming for the earthen lord.


[[Aderos]] says that there may be more to it than that, then he asks where we found it.

Luceran said we found it inside someone, and [[Leilatha]] interjects saying that it was a corrupted temple of the Wildmother. 


[[Vharkesh]] explains how the Malgrite long ago was used by a cult to try to ascend to godhood ([[Luceran]] would know that this was The Remnants, which Vharkesh has been wanting to talk to the party about.)

Midia recognizes [[Aderos]] when Vharkesh pulls her out.

[[Aderos]] warns us that the Malgrite we possess is inert but could be recharged and may still persist as a conduit of sorts. He is not going to arrest us but warns us to be careful. If we want to know more, there is a research initiative in [[1. World/Hubs/Syngorn|Syngorn]].

The Malgrite has not been seen since Errevon the Rimelord was around.

[[Davician]] was the shrine keeper or seer that looked after the shrine before it was corrupted. 

He rips a page out and tells us to convey this story to the druids of [[1. World/Hubs/Syngorn|Syngorn]] about the shrine.

Daloreth works at [[1. World/Groups/The Cobalt Soul|The Cobalt Soul]]. His desire is to seek out ancient artifacts and history to present to the Soul.


[[Luceran]] is a professor from Westhall (?) the Professor of the School of Mentalism here at the behest of Dr. Adrahan Opnotic. He's here to investigate these very such mysteries of Malgrite and what is happening here in this forest. He believes that we are in the middle of historic events.  That the Earthen Lord is being called to this plane by another party. It may be similar to the previous calamity of the Rime Lord.

When in the temple with Davician, [[Luceran]] believes he communed  with the Earthen lord, named Ka'Mort. He was controlling Davician to do his bidding.

[[Leilatha]] says her story is fairly simple. She was put up for betrothal for someone unfit and seemingly corrupt, so she left. Upon doing so, she decided to restore the temples of the Wildmother. [[Luceran]] asks if she's nobility because betrothal isn't very common. She confirms that he's close or something like that. Leilatha is concerned that her betrothed family may be involved with this somehow. 

Aderos might follow us because he has no leads?


Luceran says it seems wherever we go Adrahan has been or appeared.
