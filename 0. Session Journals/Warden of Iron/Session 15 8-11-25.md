# The Cobalt Soul Library
As we enter the Cobalt Soul Library we see two elves at a kiosk. We are surrounded by bookshelves and tables

![[Image - Party in Cobalt Soul Library.webp]]


Daloreth asks if Myralin Oakdreamer is present. 

When Elves at the kiosk say that he was marked as leaving the Cobalt Soul and is deceased.
Daloreth asks if Yuri is present and they say Yuri quit the Cobalt Soul. "He found a different passion."

Master Quain is the new head monk


![[Image - Icon on Door.webp]]




We see another adventuring party present, an Orc with his hood up, a Goblin Sorcerer coloring, a Kitsune reading a book,  and a Frog person in a trenchcoat surrounded by stacks of books taking notes. 

We see a door that says magical curses and the supernatural.  Both [[Luceran]] and [[Vharkesh]].

Vharkesh is very interested to see if he can learn anything or rituals of rebirth, undeath or cult activity. 

While Vharkesh and Luceran read their books, Daloreth goes to clear his name, reporting that he is not dead to [[1. Campaigns/2. Warden of Iron/Factions/The Cobalt Soul/Master Quain]].
Master Quain says that there were indeed reports of people here to kill Daloreth. 

I find a book titled "The Whispered One: Rumors, Rituals, and Realities."

While Luceran and myself read our books, Daloreth interacts with the other party who seems frustrated about what they are studying. Highborn Elves, who is running things. Mentions how "The Voices" run things. They are trying to find a public record of ownership and rule. "Everyone knows who these people are, there has to be money, a supplier... Maybe this isn't the library I thought it was."

I read a collection of rumors, mainly descriptions of destruction and pain left behind by what was once known as The Cult of the Whispered one. Now referred to as The Remnants - the reason they are called that is because allegedly, the leader of their cult tried to ascend to Godhood. In the same way that the Matron of Ravens did. But she had originally done the ritual, killed the death god to do so.

The Whispered One allegedly achieved godhood but then was banished beyond Divinity's Gate. 

[[1. Campaigns/2. Warden of Iron/Factions/The Remnants/The Remnants|The Remnants]] are trying to reverse the banishment. 

They would go through a town, and once they left it would be completely wasted, some would be raised in undeath and left behind. Accounts of where this has occurred is quite widespread - there are accounts of where they have been witnessed, but there is so much that it's hard to glean any pattern.

Further research yields that in records or rumors circulated, the Remnants usually set up bases in abandoned places. Places people believe to be cursed. They tend to infiltrate magical societies. They subtly push the organization to be more sympathetic to necromancy and other [[Occult]] things. Siphoning resources from the community to give to the cult itself.
