# Session Overview
## Meeting with Adrahan Optnotik
We post up to observe Luceran's meeting with professor Opnotik.

He was invited by a mysterious colleague from the past - it isn't important who he is, but he stressed that there are a great deal of new discoveries to be found in this forest. The elves opened their city and [[The League of Miracles]] were interested in strange things happening in the forest.
He asks [[Luceran]] about the mineral they have been finding (malgrite). Luceran confirms that he's come across it in his travels. When he came he was asked to join an envoy with a mission to go .... the elves were concerned about the energy suffusing the forest. where else was the mineral coming from? They did not have an answer, but they knew some were skilled enough in the forest that may know something about where it's coming from. One such person was [[Davician]]. It was one of the first people [[Luceran]] met upon his travels here.  He then explains how the temple was in ruins and corrupted.

Opnotik had a plan to meet with [[Davician]] as he had certain skills. He could feel the suffusion of magical energies from the leylines. He had refined the malgrite in some way. 

When asked how far he could reach into the leylines, he undertook a powerful ritual. A primal power where magic was not pulled from a spellbook. And it seems a primal power is what turned on him. He mentioned "The Nexus." That it was blocked.  He tried to push further but lost control. Opnotik fled at this time. But one other thing [[Davician]] mentioned was feeling a taint growing from Malanora a town southeast of the shrine. 

How did [[The League of Miracles]] not know that [[Davician]] had the control rod? (made of malgrite? Missed this part). They are the only forgers of such an item that we are aware of and control them very tightly. (((( Could the Remnants have infiltrated the league and handed it out knowing something would go wrong)))).

Apparently many have been distributed despite what happened to [[Davician]]. 

[[The League of Miracles]] is here on permission of a number of powerful elves.

[[Luceran]] tells [[Professor Opnotik]] about the Maglrite being within [[Davician]].

Luceran asks if Opnotik had heard anything of the elemental lords.

[[Luceran]] asks for [[Professor Opnotik]] to provide some contacts, but [[Professor Opnotik]] wants to come up with a cover story. He also mentions that [[The League of Miracles]] is often looking for wizardly mercenaries. 


Luceran believes that the temples of the wildmother are connected, or at least the sites of the wildmother. And they can be used as a leyline itself in a way. When trying to create the nexus. Something went terribly wrong and [[Davician]] mentioned something called 'the crypt' existed.
He is suspicious that [[Professor Opnotik]] has managed to travel around unscathed.

## To The Memory Ward
On our way to the boat service, we see a bulletin board. There are things one might expect, recruitment and such. But what stands out is a notable amount of missing persons' posters.  One clearly depicts [[Leilatha]].  She is using [[Spells/Glamorize|Glamorize]].

We discuss needing coin at some point on our travels, but [[Daloreth]] is against selling the statue, it must return to [[The Cobalt Soul]].

The ward is surrounded by a large marble wall. We see a gigantic tree that likely rests at the center of the ward. There are many steps leading up to the ward, and on each step there are written elven proverbs.

We find ourselves before the giant sequoia and it has a structure built within it. We witness the rites that occur when an elf passes, all of the memories are given to the trees for the future generations.

Dream Weavers - initiate > Dream weaver > The Voice of Memory

We enter the tree and are taken to a side room where two dreamweavers attend to us.

They ask if we can give details about the tower.  - When we admit that the tower was overrun with Malgrite, she tells her companion "my goodness, they found the tower." It was lost for a long time - the statue is one of the last visages of the commander of the order of the Living Veins - commander stargazer. Their records state that statues of their commanders were made to be commemorated - but they were lost in the Dark War. The statue contains the memory of the final days of Ilryan Stargazer. 

I ask for this person's name and she introduces her as Professor [[Ilyana Voss]]

## Into the Memory
[[Ilrien Stargazer]]
We find ourselves at the top of a tower. We are before a well armored elf with a staff who gazes out at a field and a cleared forest full of dark and cheering figures. We are completely surrounded by forces of some dark evil army.  We don't recognize their banners.
In the distance, we see what appears to be a dark ritual beginning. The commander Ilrien Stargazer turns to us and says "Welcome, what you are seeing are my final days. The  magic infused into his statue... I am but a recreation of myself. All else is as they were in the memory."

[[Leilatha]] - Why did you need to imbue your memories?
This tower should not have fallen - we were sabotaged by someone... Some double agent of sorts. all I wish to know before I return to my rest is, who was it and how did they do it?

Besides him is an ornate terminal of some sort, he explains that with that mechanism we should have been able to defend ourselves from any force. But it has been rendered useless. It is technology that was fiercely guarded, it is not well known. This is what made the order of the Living Veins so potent. A conduit into the leylines themselves. 

They are being sieged by the armies of Warren Drassig - that means we are in the time of the Shattered War. 

The ritual being performed is a summoning of shadow giants.
