# The Sabotaged Panel
![[Image - Ilrien Memory Panel.webp]] 


Warren Drassig commanded the "Dark Forces," which may comprise of the occult, shadow magic, and necromancy.

We go into the officers terminal and see a map of the leylines and the officers stop us. 

There was an explosion that took all of the instruments down three days after the ambush.

They give us a manifest of elves within the tower (may be out of date)

Elen and Ayre gave us the manifest.

Everyone on the list should still be within the tower, but there are others who are not on the list.


Down two floors we meet Beldroth Trisven - he is a recent recruit. He was brought on after the explosion. He is tasked with noting who comes in and out at the scene of the explosion. He told us every officer and each Vein tender has come by. But none understand what happened. The only veintender who hasn't come by is Shyrrik Avarren. 


We enter the store-room where the explosion occured 

![[Image - Damaged Main Line.webp]]

We see a bloodstain the size of an elven body along the wall. 

[[Daloreth]] asks about the ambush that occurred - it was on a supply run and they were reportedly attacked by their own shadows.

[[Luceran]] asks who is smeared against the wall, it was Lusha Zumrel. A few members of the tower have taken on the ancient custom of a pact of silence in mourning
Illitran Erweny and Paeral Gregwyn have taken the oath of silence. 

As we go to leave [[Vharkesh]] noticed a shimmer in the air - As I move I see my shadow is moving weirdly.  Upon investigation I see an alcove that seems off - I feel a magical energy emanating from it. I watch this energy shift and move around the room.  I investigate closer and nobody else can see what I am observing. I position myself between the channel and the shards in the room, thinking about what might have happened. An explosion of sorts. If the explosion happened outside the channel, it would have compacted, but it seems to have exploded outward and occurred within the channel. There are some magical energies I am familiar with and others I am not as they are all mixed together in a strange cloud of magic. 


We continue on to seek out some Vein Tenders who might know more. 

As we try to leave a Shadow Eel grapples Luceran - I bust the door down and try to free him. 

We defeat one, but the other says they are watching us. The elf did not see any of this, it seems only we can see them.


# The Residential Floor
The next floor down, we meet a woman named Shyrrik Avarren  and when we ask about what happened with the explosion she explains that she already told [[Ilrien Stargazer]] everything she knew and she only saw Arnarra and a couple Vein Tenders who shouldn't have even been there, specifically Illitran Erweny. The Vein was her duty that day but she was not able to tend to the leyline before the explosion occurred. That day she heard there was trouble on the floors below, she isn't sure what it was because by the time she got there nothing was going on.

[[Vharkesh]] believes that she was drawn away to allow access to the leyline as she is an easy person to pin it on.

She said that Arnarra alerted her to interesting things that led her away initially. Vague things going wrong for Shyrrik to investigate. When she returned, Arnarra and Illitran leaving the siphoning room. Beyond that, she didn't notice anything out of the ordinary. One of the siphoners wasn't fully reset, but that is not too uncommon. 

She says we could find either of them - Arnarra is probably sweeping the lower levels as it is her job. But she isn't sure where Illitran would be, she doesn't know his duties today. 

We want to investigate the meeting room because it is right below the siphoning room. Vharkesh also wants to question the 'new vein tender' in the other room just to get their perspective on things.
