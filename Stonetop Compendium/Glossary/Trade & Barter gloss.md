Commonly available items - things on the Inventory insert, or that could be found in pretty much any Stonetop household - are readily available. Folks trade, loan, and gift these items all the time, and its not worth tracking them. See page 95 for a list of example **common items.**

To acquire or sell a special item, though, you must roll to [[Trade & Barter gloss]] (page 83). The result tells you whether you can make the trade easily and fairly (on a 10+), or if there's a catch (on a 7-9), or if the trade just isn't going to happen here and now.

See page 96 for a list of **special items.** For items not on the list, the GM decides whether there's any chance of finding it (or finding a buyer for it). If not, don't roll to [[Trade & Barter gloss]]; [[Make a Plan]] (page 81) with the GM instead.
