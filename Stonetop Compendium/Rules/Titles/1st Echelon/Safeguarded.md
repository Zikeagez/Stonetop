#### Safeguarded

*They risk their lives for me because I risk my life for theirs. This fight belongs to all of us!*

**Prerequisite:** You earn a noble rank or earn the favor of a knight's guild.

**Effect:** You start combat encounters with a squad of three minions from the specific monster band or people you earned this title from. The squad takes their turn as a part of your turn. On the squad's turn, they can take a move action and either a main action or maneuver.

**Special:** If two or more heroes in the party have this title from the same source, the party starts combat encounters with a squad of six minions instead. At the start of each round of combat, the heroes decide who controls the squad as a part of their turn.
