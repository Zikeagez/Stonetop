### 9th-Level Features
As a 9th-level beastheart, you gain the following features.

#### Avatar of the green
Your companion has transcended beasthood. Although they're still your faithful friend, they're also a vessel for nature's wisdom and memories. Your companion's Reason score increases to 1, or increases by 1 if it is already 1 or higher, and they learn every language you know. Your companion can communicate telepathically with any creature within 10 squares, using language as well as images and feelings. Additionally, you learn the Nature skill and one other skill from the lore group.

#### 9th-Level Wild Nature Ability
Your wild nature grants your choice of one of two heroic abilities.
