*A gaunlet of loose chains rattles, shackles to each of the user's fingers. They clatter whenever magic surges through them.*

**Keywords**:  Arms, Magic

**Item Prerequisite**: Blood-soaked chains that restrained a demon for at least 30 days.

**Project Source**: Texts or lore in Tholl

**Project Roll Characteristic**: Reason or Presence

**Project Goal**: 300

**Effect:**  While wearing this gauntlet, your ranged free strikes have the Magic keyword and if the target has `P < AVERAGE`, they are magically chained to you until the end of their next turn. While chained, the target can't move farther away from you, and you can use a maneuver to either vertically pull the target up to 3 squares or shift yourself up to 3 squares through the air towards the target.

**Special:** If you are a summoner, you regain the use of your standard ranged free strike while wearing this gauntlet.
