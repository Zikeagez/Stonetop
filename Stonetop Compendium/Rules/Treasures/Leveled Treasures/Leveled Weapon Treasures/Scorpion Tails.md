*These braids of articulated, wickedly barbed tails weave behind your head, threatening painful stings.*

**Keywords**: Light Weapon, Magic

**Item Prerequisite**: The stinger from a manticore's tail

**Project Source**: Texts or lore in Khemharic

**Project Roll Characteristics**: Agility, Reason, or Intuition

**1st Level**: Only a beastheart can wield this weapon. Any weapon ability that deals rolled damage using this weapon deals an extra 1 poison damage. Additionally, you and your companion can use the following maneuver.


```ds-feature

  - Strike

effects:
  - name: Effect
    effect: The target takes poison damage equal to 3 + your Might score
```




**5th Level**: The weapon's extra damage increases to 2. Additionally, the Scorpion Tail ability's distance increases to melee 3.

**9th Level**:  The weapon's extra damage increases to 3. Additionally, the Scorpion Tail ability's distance increases to melee 4.
