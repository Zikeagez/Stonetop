>(Requires Spirit Tongue)
>When ***a spirit beast loans you power,*** ask the GM for one of its tags or moves. Store it in your pouch in place of 1 Stock. When you ***use the borrowed tag or move,*** roll +WIS: **on a 10+,** you do it and can use the power again; **on a 7-9,** you do it, but lose the power.
