#### Arcane Trick

You have the following ability.

[[Stonetop Compendium/Rules/Abilities/Perk Abilities/Arcane Trick|Arcane Trick]]
```ds-feature
type: feature
feature_type: ability
name: Arcane Trick
cost: 
flavor: You cast an entertaining spell that creates a minor but impressive magical effect.
keywords:
  - Magic
usage: Main action
distance: Self
target: 🎯Self
effects:
  - name: Effect 
    effect: Choose one of the following effects...
  - name: Teleport object
    effect: You teleport a size 1S or smaller object adjacent to you into an unoccupied space adjacent to you.
  - name: Sparks
    effect: Until the start of your next turn, a part of your body shoots a shower of harmless noisy sparks that light up each square adjacent to you.
  - name: Flavor
    effect: You transform up to 1 pound of edible food you touch to make it taste delicious or disgusting.
  - name: Ignite or Snuff Light
    effect: You ignite or snuff out (your choice) every mundane light source of 1L or smaller adjacent to you.
  - name: Odor
    effect: Until the start of your next turn, you make your body exude a particular odor you've smelled before. This smell can be sensed by each creature within 5 squares of you, but can't impose any condition or other drawback on those creatures.
  - name: Inscription
    effect: You place a small magical inscription on the surface of a mundane object you touch, or you can remove an inscription that was made by you or by another creature using Arcane Trick.
  - name: Illusion
    effect: You touch a size 1T object to cover it with an illusion that makes it look like a different object. Any creature who handles the object becomes aware of the illusion. The illusion ends when you stop touching the object.
```
