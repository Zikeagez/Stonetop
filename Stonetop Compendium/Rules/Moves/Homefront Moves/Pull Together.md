When you ***set a community to work on improvements, to secure new resources, or to make major repairs,*** spend whatever the GM says is required (time, material, Surplus, etc.) and roll +Population: **on a 10+,** the job gets done; **on a 7-9,** pick 1:

- It gets done, but other work doesn't; reduce Fortunes by 1
- It gets done, but the work is shoddy, crude
- It gets done, but there's a consequence (bad blood, an injury, a thread unearthed, etc.)
- There's an unforeseen cost, requirement, or challenge; address it and the job gets done.

for more information, see page 536.
