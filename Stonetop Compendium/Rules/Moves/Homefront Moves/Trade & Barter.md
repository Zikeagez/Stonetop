When you ***wish to acquire or sell a commonly available item,*** you can. When you ***seek to acquire or sell a special item,*** roll +Prosperity and subtract the item's Value. In winter, you have disadvantage. **On a 10+,** you can get it or sell it for a fair price; **on a 7-9 when you're trying to buy,** the GM picks 1:

- You can get it, but it'll cost more than usual
- ___ has it, but they aren't keen to give it up
- You can get something close, but not quite right

**On a 7-9 when you're looking to sell:**
you can sell it now, but you won't get its full worth.

**On a 6- either way,** don't mark XP. If you want to acquire/sell it, you'll need to travel to ___ or wait until next season, and then try again.

For unique or truly exceptional items, don't Trade & Barter. Either get with the GM and [[Make a Plan]] or wait for the trade opportunity when Seasons Change.
