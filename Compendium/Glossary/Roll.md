Many moves tell you to "roll +STAT" (usually with a specific stat, like STR or INT, see page 52).

"Roll" means: roll wo six-sided dice (2d6), and add them together.

"+STAT" means: find that stat in your PC's playbook and add it to the roll. Stats range from -1 to +3.
