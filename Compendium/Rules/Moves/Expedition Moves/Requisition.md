>When you ***borrow some of the steading's assets for an expedition*** (like the horses or a plow), roll +Fortunes: on a 10+, og ahead, but bring it back safely; on a 7-9, you'll need to do some convincing; on a 6-, don't mark XP - you can take the asset with you if you want, but if you do, reduce Fortunes by 1.

For more information, see 308.
