>When you ***prepare for an expedition in a friendly community,*** mark as many ◇ on your Inventory insert as you wish to carry, either on specific items or in "undefined." Mark up to 3◇ for a *light* load (quick and quiet), 4-6 ◇ for a *normal* load, or 7-9 ◇ for *heavy* load (noisy, slow, quick to tire). Also, mark a number of ▢ in the **Small items** section equal to 4+ Prosperity (on specific items in "undefined").
>
>You can elect...
>
>- Items printed on the Inventory insert
>- Other common, mundane items
>- Any of your personal possessions
>- Special items for which you Trade & Barter

Tell the GM what you're bringing, and answer their questions about your gear and where you got it.
