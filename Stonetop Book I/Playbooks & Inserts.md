# Playbooks
When you create a PC for *Stonetop,* the first thing you'll do is pick your playbook. Each playbook contains a set of choices, prompts, and options that point towards a specific character archetype, while leaving room to make your character unique.

your playbook also serves as a character sheet. You'll use it to track your Pc's stats, debilities, current HP and XP, moves, and so forth. The playbooks are designed to be printed double-sided and folded down the middle, so that they form a little booklet.

The nine standard playbooks are:

- **[[The Blessed]]** (page 105)
- **[[The Fox]]** (page 109)
- **[[The Heavy]]** (page 113)
- **[[The Judge]]** (page 117)
- **[[The Lightbearer]]** (page 121)
- **[[The Marshal]]** (page 125)
- **[[The Ranger]]** (page 129)
- **[[The Seeker]]** (page 133)
- **[[The Would-be Hero]]** (page 137)

For an explanation of each component of a Pc playbook, see [[Playing the Game#Your character|Your character]] (page 50) in the previous chapter.

Each PC also has at least one **insert.** The standard inserts are also included in this chapter, starting on page 141.

When you have questions about particular moves or other elements of a playbook (how they work, what exactly ___ means, what constitutes "a creature of darkness," etc.), talk it out with the GM and the other players. Consider what's already been established or implied, and work it out as a group.

Don't be surprised if the GM turns such questions back on you. "Well, what do *you* think constitutes a creature of darkness?" This isn't a trap or a trick! it's an invitation for you to **help author the world.** Answer with whatever seems obvious or interesting to you. Just be prepared for that answer to have lasting implications for how the move works in your game!

# Inserts

Your PC will have at least one insert, a double-sided half-page sheet that you can slip inside your folded playbook. PDFs are included in the playkit on the Lampblack & Brimstone website, but the inserts are also included in this chapter for reference.

Every PC gets the **Inventory** sheet (page 142). See **[[Playing the Game#Gear and possessions|Gear and possessions]]** on page 86 for an explanation on how this insert is used. The back of the insert will vary based on your playbook and choices made.

If you are ...

... **[[The Ranger]] with the [[Animal Companion]] move,** then you get the **Animal Companion insert,** (page 143), of course;

... **[[The Marshal]]**, then you get the **Crew insert** (page 144);

... **[[The Blessed]] with the [[Initiate]] background,** then you get the **Initiates of Danu,** insert (page 145);

... **the Lightbearer,** then you get the **Invocations insert**  (page 146);

Otherwise, you get a **generic insert** (page 147) with space for tracking followers and a summary of your player agenda, principles and rules.

The PDF in the playkit is set up to print with the Inventory sheet on one page and the playbook-specific (or generic follower / agenda sheets) on the back. Only one copy of each sheet is included in this chapter.

There are also three inserts that can be gained as a result of the [[Death's Door]] move and bad decision-making: the **Ghost** (page 148), the **Revenant** (page 150), and the **Threall** (page 152). These inserts are normally printed out double-sided on a half-sheet of paper. You take one of them only when it becomes relevant.

In addition to the inserts presented in this chapter, you might also get an insert that represents a **major arcanum** (page 60). [[The Seeker]] starts play with a major arcanum as indicated by their background; and if you take [[The Heavy]] playbook with the "[[Storm-Marked]]" background, you start play with the Storm Markings major arcanum. You'll find these in Appendix D: Major Arcana (Book II, page 540).

Some inserts will not be notated in markdown in this vault due to their formatting, please refer to their PDF file.
